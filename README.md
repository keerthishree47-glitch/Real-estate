# Real-estate

Created a Power BI dashboard to look at real estate listings sales results, details about properties how agents are performing and how investments are doing. The work included taking real estate data and using **Power Query, DAX and Power BI** to make sense of it and find business information.

Main areas looked at:
1.Properties that are for sale how much they are sold for. What kind of properties they are
2.How well cities, states and different parts of the market are doing
3.How much properties are listed for versus how much they actually sell for and how long they stay on the market
4.How agents and branches are doing
5.How many people look at properties how many ask questions and where those leads come from
6.How many properties are occupied how many are empty how money they bring in from rent and how much cash flow they generate each year
7.How much income is left after expenses what the capitalization rate is and what the return, on investment is to see how well investments are performing
8.Interactive tools and key performance indicators so that business decisions can be made quickly

Tools and skills used:
Power BI 
Power Query 
DAX  
Excel 
Data Cleaning 
Data Modeling 
Data Visualization 
Business Analysis
